Dental disease is one of the most common health problems in dogs and cats. Tartar and bacteria can cause bad breath, painful gums, loose teeth, and infections that spread to other organs. A professional dental cleaning is the best way to deal with it.

A dental cleaning at PawCare costs $320 and takes about 90 minutes. It is performed under general anesthesia, which allows us to clean thoroughly below the gum line, polish the teeth, and examine every tooth safely. Anesthesia also keeps your pet comfortable and still.

Because of the anesthesia, the same preparation as for surgery applies. Remove food at 10 pm the night before, allow water until 6 am, and bring your pet at the drop-off time, which is between 7:30 and 8:00 am. Pickup is usually between 4 and 6 pm on the same day. Please read "Preparing for Surgery" for full details.

Because the bill is over $300, you may choose to pay in 3 monthly installments with no interest. PawCare Plus members receive one annual dental cleaning as part of their plan. See "Wellness Plans".

After the cleaning, your pet may be a little sleepy and its throat slightly sore. Offer soft food that evening. Between cleanings, you can help your pet by brushing its teeth several times per week with a pet-safe toothpaste. Never use human toothpaste.
