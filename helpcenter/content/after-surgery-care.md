---
title: After Surgery Care
category: Surgery & Recovery
---

Most pets go home the same afternoon, usually between 4 and 6 pm, still a little groggy. Your pet may be sleepy, wobbly, or less hungry that evening, which is normal. Offer a small meal and some water, and keep your pet in a quiet, warm room.

For the next 10 to 14 days, your pet must wear an e-collar (the plastic cone) to prevent licking or chewing at the incision. This is the most important rule of recovery, because licking can open the stitches and cause infection. Keep the collar on even when you think your pet is not bothered by the wound, and also at night.

Other care instructions:
- Limit activity. Keep dogs on a short leash for bathroom breaks, and do not allow running, jumping, or rough play. Keep cats indoors.
- No bathing for 14 days. Keep the incision dry and clean.
- Check the incision twice a day. A little redness is expected, but contact us if you see swelling, discharge, an opened wound, or a bad smell.
- Give any pain medication exactly as prescribed, and never give human painkillers.

Stitches removal is done at day 10 to 14 after surgery and is free. Please book the visit when you pick up your pet or call us at (555) 014-7788.

Call us right away if your pet vomits repeatedly, will not eat or drink for more than 24 hours, seems very weak, or has pale gums. If this happens when we are closed, call the Springfield 24h Animal ER at (555) 019-2400, 300 Harbor Road. PawCare does not operate a 24-hour emergency service.
