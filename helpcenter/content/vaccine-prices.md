---
title: Vaccine Prices
category: Vaccines & Prevention
---

Vaccines at PawCare are given during a vaccination visit. The visit fee is $45, and each vaccine is charged separately at the price below. A vaccination visit takes about 30 minutes.

- Rabies: $28
- DHPP (distemper, hepatitis, parainfluenza, parvovirus), dogs: $35
- FVRCP (feline distemper combination), cats: $32
- Leptospirosis, dogs: $30
- Bordetella, dogs: $26
- Feline leukemia (FeLV), cats: $38

For example, an adult dog receiving rabies and DHPP would pay $45 for the visit plus $28 and $35 for the vaccines, a total of $108. An adult cat receiving rabies and FVRCP would pay $45 plus $28 and $32, a total of $105.

Which vaccines your pet needs depends on its age, lifestyle, and vaccine history. Rabies, DHPP for dogs, and FVRCP for cats are considered core vaccines. Leptospirosis, bordetella, and FeLV are recommended based on risk, for example if your dog boards or visits groomers and parks, or if your cat goes outdoors. Your veterinarian will help you choose.

If your pet is also being seen for a wellness exam, you can combine both. The wellness exam is $65 and the vaccination visit fee applies in addition, so ask the front desk when booking if you want both in the same appointment. Members of our wellness plans have core vaccines included. See "Wellness Plans".

Puppies and kittens follow a series of shots. See "Puppy Vaccination Schedule" and "Kitten Vaccination Schedule".
