title: Flea, Tick and Heartworm Prevention
category: Vaccines & Prevention
---

Prevention is easier, cheaper, and safer than treating a problem after it appears. We recommend monthly flea and tick prevention and monthly heartworm prevention year-round for all dogs. Giving products only in summer leaves gaps, because fleas can survive indoors and ticks can be active on mild winter days.

Heartworm disease is spread by mosquitoes and can seriously damage the heart and lungs. It is much easier to prevent than to treat. Please give the preventive on the same day every month, and set a reminder on your phone.

Cats need protection too. Even indoor cats still need flea prevention, because fleas can enter the home on people, on clothing, or on other pets. Your veterinarian can recommend the right product for your cat's weight and age. Never use a dog flea product on a cat, as some ingredients are toxic to cats.

Puppies and kittens also need deworming. We deworm every 2 weeks until 12 weeks of age, and then follow a schedule based on your pet's lifestyle. See "Puppy Vaccination Schedule" and "Kitten Vaccination Schedule".

To get started, book a wellness exam ($65). The veterinarian will weigh your pet, discuss lifestyle and risks, and recommend a product. To get a prescription-only preventive, your pet needs an exam within the last 12 months. See "Prescriptions and Records".

Signs of fleas include scratching, small black specks in the fur, and skin irritation. If you find a tick, remove it with fine-tipped tweezers close to the skin and call us if the area becomes red or your pet seems unwell.
