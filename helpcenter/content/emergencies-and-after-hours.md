Important: PawCare does not run a 24-hour emergency room. When we are closed, which means evenings, Sundays, and Saturdays after 13:00, please call or go to the Springfield 24h Animal ER at 300 Harbor Road, phone (555) 019-2400.

During our opening hours (Monday to Friday 9:00 to 18:00, Saturday 9:00 to 13:00), emergencies that walk in are triaged first, ahead of routine appointments. If you are on your way, call us at (555) 014-7788 so that the team can prepare. Keep your pet calm and, if possible, in a carrier or wrapped in a blanket.

Go to an emergency vet immediately if you notice any of these warning signs:
- Difficulty breathing, open-mouth breathing in cats, or blue or pale gums
- Suspected poisoning, for example chocolate, xylitol (a sweetener found in sugar-free gum and some peanut butters), grapes or raisins, or lilies for cats
- A swollen, hard, or bloated abdomen, especially with retching
- Seizures
- A male cat straining to urinate or not urinating at all
- Major bleeding or a serious injury, such as being hit by a car
- Collapse, extreme weakness, or unresponsiveness

If you think your pet has eaten something toxic, do not wait for symptoms to appear and do not try to make your pet vomit unless a veterinarian tells you to. Bring the packaging, or note what and how much was eaten.

When in doubt, call. It is always better to ask than to wait.
