title: Wellness Plans
category: Prices & Payment
---

PawCare offers two monthly wellness plans that spread the cost of routine care over the year. Both require a 12-month commitment.

PawCare Basic costs $29 per month. It includes 2 wellness exams per year, core vaccines, and 10% off our other services.

PawCare Plus costs $49 per month. It includes everything in Basic, plus one annual dental cleaning, and a bigger discount of 15% off other services.

To see how this works in practice, a wellness exam costs $65 and a dental cleaning costs $320 when paid per visit. With Plus, the annual dental cleaning is covered by the plan, and any other services, such as a nail trim or a microchip, receive the 15% discount. Basic is a good fit for healthy pets that need regular checkups and their vaccines. Plus is a good fit for pets that are likely to need a dental cleaning, such as adult and senior dogs and cats.

The discount applies to other services at PawCare, not to services already included in your plan. If your pet needs something outside the plan, your veterinarian will always explain the cost first.

Because the plans have a 12-month commitment, we recommend thinking about your pet's needs for the whole year before you sign up. To enroll, ask at the front desk, call (555) 014-7788, or talk to the AI assistant on our website. Monthly fees are paid by card.
