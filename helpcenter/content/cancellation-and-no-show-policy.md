---
title: Cancellation and No-Show Policy
category: Policies
---

We know plans change, and we want to make it easy to adjust your appointment. You can cancel or reschedule for free as long as you do so at least 24 hours before your appointment time.

If you cancel or reschedule less than 24 hours before the appointment, or if you do not show up, a $25 fee applies. The fee is charged at your next visit. Our schedule is built around fixed time slots, and a late cancellation usually means that another pet loses the chance to be seen that day.

To cancel or reschedule, you can use the same channels you used to book: online, through the AI assistant on our website, or by calling us at (555) 014-7788 during opening hours. Since we are closed on Sundays, keep in mind that a Monday morning appointment needs to be changed by the Saturday before at the same hour, not on Sunday. Messages left outside opening hours count from the time we receive them.

Surgeries and dental cleanings are a special case because they involve an anesthesia team and a prepared surgical room. If you need to postpone one of these, please call us as soon as you know, ideally several days in advance, so that we can offer the slot to someone else.

If something unexpected happens, such as a sudden illness of your own or a true emergency with your pet, please call us. The $25 fee is a general rule, but we are happy to talk about the circumstances.
