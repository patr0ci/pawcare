---
title: First Visit: What to Bring
category: Visits & Booking
---

A first visit to PawCare is usually a wellness exam, which costs $65 and takes about 30 minutes. A little preparation helps us give your pet the best care and keeps the appointment relaxed.

Please bring your pet's previous vaccine records. If your pet was adopted from a shelter or seen by another clinic, ask them to send the records, or bring any papers you received at adoption. These records let us avoid repeating vaccines and let us plan what is due next. If you cannot find them, bring what you have and we will work out a plan with you.

Other things to bring:
- A list of any medications, supplements, and the food your pet eats, including brand and amount.
- Notes on anything that worries you, such as changes in appetite, drinking, energy, or bathroom habits.
- A stool sample if you can collect a fresh one that day, in a sealed bag.
- Your payment method. Payment is due at the end of the visit.

Dogs must be on a leash and all other animals in a carrier. A familiar blanket or a few treats can help nervous pets feel safer. For cats, leave the carrier open at home a few days before the visit so that it feels like a normal place to rest.

Please arrive 10 minutes early so that we can set up your pet's file at the front desk. If you do not know which veterinarian to see, Dr. Maya Chen handles general care for dogs and cats, and Dr. Lena Ortiz sees rabbits, birds, and cats.
