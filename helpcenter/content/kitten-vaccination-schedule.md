title: Kitten Vaccination Schedule
category: Vaccines & Prevention
---

Kittens need a series of vaccines in their first months to build solid protection. Here is our standard schedule.

- 8 weeks: FVRCP (first dose)
- 12 weeks: FVRCP (second dose)
- 16 weeks: FVRCP (third dose) and rabies
- Feline leukemia (FeLV): 2 doses, given 3 to 4 weeks apart

FeLV vaccination is especially recommended for kittens, and for any cat that goes outdoors or lives with cats whose status is unknown. We usually give the first FeLV dose together with one of the early FVRCP visits and the second dose 3 to 4 weeks later.

Each dose is given at a vaccination visit, which costs $45 plus the vaccines: FVRCP is $32, rabies is $28, and FeLV is $38 per dose.

Kittens should also be dewormed every 2 weeks until 12 weeks of age. Your veterinarian will check the schedule at each visit. Flea prevention is important too, even for kittens that will live entirely indoors, and the veterinarian will advise on the right product for the kitten's weight and age.

Keep your kitten indoors and away from unknown cats until the series is complete. A calm, quiet room with a litter box, food, water, and a hiding place helps a new kitten settle in.
