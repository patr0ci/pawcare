---
title: How to Book an Appointment
category: Visits & Booking
---

There are three ways to book an appointment at PawCare: online, by phone, or through the AI assistant on our website. All three show the same availability, so you can use whichever is most convenient for you.

To book online or with the AI assistant, choose the service you need, such as a wellness exam, a vaccination visit, a dental cleaning, or a nail trim. Then pick a veterinarian and a time. If you are not sure which service fits your pet, the assistant can help you decide. To book by phone, call (555) 014-7788 during opening hours: Monday to Friday from 9:00 to 18:00 and Saturday from 9:00 to 13:00.

Please keep appointment lengths in mind when choosing a slot. A wellness exam takes about 30 minutes, a vaccination visit about 30 minutes, a nail trim about 15 minutes, a dental cleaning about 90 minutes, and a spay or neuter surgery about 60 minutes. Surgeries and dental cleanings require special preparation, so please read our preparation guides before the day.

When booking, tell us your pet's species. Rabbits and birds are seen only by Dr. Lena Ortiz, who also sees cats. Dogs and cats can see Dr. Maya Chen for general care or Dr. Rafael Souza for surgery. We do not see reptiles.

You will get a confirmation with the date, time, and veterinarian. Please arrive 10 minutes early. If you need to cancel or reschedule, do it at least 24 hours ahead to avoid a fee. If this is your first visit, see "First Visit: What to Bring".
