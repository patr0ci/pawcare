This page lists our main services with their prices in US dollars and typical appointment lengths. Vaccine prices are listed separately in "Vaccine Prices".

- Wellness exam: $65 (30 minutes)
- Vaccination visit: $45 plus the price of each vaccine given (30 minutes)
- Dental cleaning: $320 (90 minutes, performed under anesthesia)
- Spay or neuter, cat: $280 (60 minutes)
- Spay or neuter, dog under 20 kg: $380 (60 minutes)
- Spay or neuter, dog 20 kg or more: $460 (60 minutes)
- Microchip: $50
- Nail trim: $20 (15 minutes)

A follow-up visit within 14 days of a paid exam is free. This covers rechecks related to the same problem, for example to see how your pet is responding to treatment. A free stitches removal visit after surgery is also included in the surgery price.

Please note that these prices are for the service itself. If your pet needs medications, tests, or additional treatment, your veterinarian will explain the options and costs before going ahead. You will never be charged for extra work without your agreement.

Payment is due at the end of the visit. We accept credit and debit cards, cash, and Apple Pay or Google Pay. For bills over $300, such as a dental cleaning or a large-dog spay, you can split the payment into 3 monthly installments with no interest. See "Payment Methods, Payment Plans and Pet Insurance" for details. If you plan to use our wellness plans, ask about the discounts for other services in "Wellness Plans".
