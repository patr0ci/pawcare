---
title: Preparing for Surgery
category: Surgery & Recovery
---

Good preparation makes surgery safer and recovery smoother. This guide applies to spays, neuters, and other procedures with anesthesia, including dental cleanings. Dr. Rafael Souza performs our surgeries on dogs and cats.

Fasting is essential. Please remove your pet's food at 10 pm the night before surgery. Water is allowed until 6 am on the morning of the procedure, and then should be taken away. An empty stomach lowers the risk of vomiting while your pet is under anesthesia. If your pet takes regular medication or is very young, small, or diabetic, call us before the fast so we can give specific instructions.

Drop-off is between 7:30 and 8:00 am. You will meet a team member, review the consent form, and confirm the procedure and a phone number where we can reach you during the day. Please bring your pet on a leash or in a carrier.

Prices for common procedures are $280 for a cat spay or neuter, $380 for a dog under 20 kg, and $460 for a dog of 20 kg or more. Payment is due at pickup, and bills over $300 can be split into 3 monthly installments with no interest.

Before the surgery, we recommend a bath a day or two earlier, because your pet cannot be bathed for 14 days afterward. Also prepare a quiet, clean, comfortable place at home for recovery.

Pickup is usually between 4 and 6 pm on the same day. We will call you when your pet is awake, and we will go over home care with you. See "After Surgery Care".
