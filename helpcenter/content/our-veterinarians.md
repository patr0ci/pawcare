PawCare has three veterinarians, each with a different focus. Knowing who does what helps you book the right appointment.

Dr. Maya Chen is our general practice veterinarian for dogs and cats. She handles wellness exams, vaccinations, preventive care, and the diagnosis and treatment of everyday health problems such as skin issues, digestive upset, and limping. If you are unsure whom to see for a dog or a cat, Dr. Chen is the best starting point.

Dr. Rafael Souza is our surgeon for dogs and cats. He performs spays and neuters as well as other surgical procedures, and he oversees the surgical team on operation days. After a surgery, he or the team checks the incision during your free stitches removal visit.

Dr. Lena Ortiz is our exotics veterinarian. She treats rabbits and birds, and she also sees cats. All bird patients are seen only by Dr. Ortiz, and rabbits are seen by her as well. Because her schedule is more limited, we recommend booking rabbit and bird appointments a little in advance.

When you book online, by phone at (555) 014-7788, or through the AI assistant on our website, you can pick a veterinarian directly. If you do not have a preference, the assistant or our front desk will match your pet to the right doctor.

PawCare does not treat reptiles. For reptiles, we refer you to Springfield Exotic Animal Hospital.
