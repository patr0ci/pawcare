PawCare is a small-animal clinic. We see dogs, cats, rabbits, and birds. Each species is cared for by the veterinarian best suited to it.

Dogs and cats can see Dr. Maya Chen for general practice, including wellness exams, vaccines, and illness, and Dr. Rafael Souza for surgery. Cats may also see Dr. Lena Ortiz.

Rabbits are seen by Dr. Lena Ortiz, our exotics veterinarian. Birds are seen only by Dr. Ortiz. If you have a rabbit or a bird, please book with her specifically, and book a little in advance because her appointment times are limited.

We do not treat reptiles, such as snakes, lizards, turtles, or tortoises. For reptiles, we refer you to Springfield Exotic Animal Hospital. We are sorry we cannot help with these animals, and we recommend that you contact them directly.

Services and prices can differ by species. The prices on our services page apply to dogs and cats as listed. For rabbits and birds, please call us at (555) 014-7788 or ask the AI assistant so that we can confirm the right service for your pet.

When you come to the clinic, dogs must be on a leash, and all other animals must be in a carrier. Birds and rabbits should be transported in a secure carrier, covered with a light cloth to keep them calm and protected from drafts.

If your pet is not on this list, call us and we will gladly point you to the right place.
