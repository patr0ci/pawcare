---
title: Opening Hours and Location
category: Visits & Booking
---

PawCare Veterinary Clinic is located at 1420 Maple Avenue, Springfield. You can reach our front desk at (555) 014-7788 during opening hours.

Our regular hours are Monday through Friday from 9:00 to 18:00 and Saturday from 9:00 to 13:00. We are closed on Sundays. Appointments are scheduled within these hours, and the last appointment slots of the day are usually the shorter services such as nail trims and vaccination visits.

Surgery patients are the one exception to the opening hours: on surgery days, drop-off takes place between 7:30 and 8:00 am so that the veterinary team can prepare before the doors open to the public. You will receive the exact instructions when your surgery is booked.

PawCare does not run a 24-hour emergency service. If your pet needs urgent care when we are closed, please call the Springfield 24h Animal ER at (555) 019-2400, located at 300 Harbor Road. During opening hours, emergencies that walk in are triaged first, ahead of routine appointments.

A few practical tips for your visit:
- Please arrive about 10 minutes before your appointment time so that check-in does not eat into your pet's visit.
- Dogs must be on a leash and all other pets must be in a carrier when they enter the building.
- If you are running late, call us at (555) 014-7788. We will do our best to keep your slot, but a significantly late arrival may need to be rescheduled.
