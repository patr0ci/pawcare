title: Prescriptions and Records
category: Policies
---

Prescription refills: to request a refill for your pet's medication, your pet must have had an exam at PawCare within the last 12 months. This rule helps us make sure the medication is still the right choice and that your pet is being monitored. If it has been more than 12 months, book a wellness exam ($65) and the veterinarian can renew the prescription during the visit.

To request a refill, call us at (555) 014-7788 during opening hours or ask the AI assistant on our website. Please have the name of the medication and your pet's name ready, and allow some time for the request to be processed rather than waiting until the last dose. Refill requests are easier to handle on weekdays, as the clinic is open on Saturday only until 13:00 and closed on Sunday.

Medical records: you are entitled to a copy of your pet's records. Send your request by email or ask the front desk, and we answer records requests within 2 business days by email. Records can include exam notes, vaccine history, lab results, and invoices. If you are moving to another clinic, give us the other clinic's name and email, and we can send the records directly.

Itemized invoices for pet insurance claims can be requested in the same way. See "Payment Methods, Payment Plans and Pet Insurance".

If you are bringing a new pet to PawCare, bring or send the previous vaccine records before the first visit. This helps us to build the right vaccine and prevention plan. If you cannot locate them, tell us at the visit and we will plan accordingly.
