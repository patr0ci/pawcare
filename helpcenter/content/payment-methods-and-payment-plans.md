---
title: Payment Methods, Payment Plans and Pet Insurance
category: Prices & Payment
---

Payment at PawCare is due at the end of the visit, once you have seen the final invoice. For surgeries, payment is due when you pick up your pet. We accept credit and debit cards, cash, and Apple Pay or Google Pay.

For bills over $300, you can choose a payment plan: 3 monthly installments with no interest. For example, a dental cleaning ($320) or a spay of a dog over 20 kg ($460) qualifies. The first installment is paid at the visit and the other two are charged monthly afterward to the card you register. Please ask the front desk before you pay so that we can set up the plan.

Many clients also use pet insurance. PawCare does not bill insurance companies directly. Instead, we provide an itemized invoice listing each service, vaccine, and medication, and you file the claim with your insurer yourself. You pay the clinic at the end of the visit, then submit the invoice to your insurance company for reimbursement according to your policy.

Tips for insurance claims:
- Ask for the itemized invoice before you leave, or request it by email afterward.
- Check with your insurer which documents they need, such as medical records. We answer records requests within 2 business days by email.
- Keep in mind that policies differ on pre-existing conditions, waiting periods, and what share of the bill is reimbursed. Only your insurer can confirm what your plan covers.

If you are interested in spreading routine costs over the year, our wellness plans may also help. See "Wellness Plans". Questions about a bill can be sent to us by phone at (555) 014-7788.
