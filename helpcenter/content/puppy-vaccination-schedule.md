title: Puppy Vaccination Schedule
category: Vaccines & Prevention

Puppies are protected by antibodies from their mother only for a short time, so they need a series of vaccines to build their own immunity. Our standard puppy schedule is simple to follow.

- 8 weeks: DHPP (first dose)
- 12 weeks: DHPP (second dose)
- 16 weeks: DHPP (third dose) and rabies

Each dose is given at a vaccination visit, which costs $45 plus the price of the vaccines: DHPP is $35 and rabies is $28. Leptospirosis ($30) and bordetella ($26) can be added if your puppy will be around other dogs, at daycare, or at the groomer. Ask your veterinarian during the first visit.

Puppies also need deworming. We deworm every 2 weeks until the puppy is 12 weeks old. Your veterinarian will tell you when the next dose is due, and you can combine it with the vaccination visits.

Until your puppy has completed the series at 16 weeks, it is wise to avoid places where many unknown dogs gather, such as dog parks, because the puppy is not fully protected yet. Gentle socialization with healthy, vaccinated dogs and with new people at home is still very beneficial.

Bring any vaccine records you received from the breeder or shelter to the first visit. If your puppy already had a shot before coming to you, we will adjust the schedule accordingly.
